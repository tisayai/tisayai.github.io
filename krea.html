How the atelier works
Character creation happens locally in your browser without an API key. The output is an editable written prompt for Krea 2.
Tips for using Krea 2
  • Begin with clear subject identity and species anatomy.
  • Use meaningful visual anchors, clothing construction, composition, and lighting.
  • Choose Exploratory for variety or Art Director for deliberate styling.
  • Use a reference image inside Krea for stronger appearance consistency.
  • Reroll targeted details instead of replacing an established character.

Species-aware design supports catkin, foxkin, wolfkin, angels, demons, vampires, merfolk, yokai, and more.
