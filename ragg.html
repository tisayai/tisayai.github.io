About Aetheria

Aetheria lies between the Sapphire Expanse and the cloudbound northern frontier. Its kingdoms were shaped by celestial storms, living forests, merchant guilds, and the ruins of a civilization that vanished beneath the mountains.

  • A living world Seasons, trade, and regional conflicts shape every expedition.
