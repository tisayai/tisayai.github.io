How summoning works
Every portrait is composed locally in your browser. Genre rules, seedword concepts, hand-selected traits, and controlled variety shape a complete natural-language illustration prompt. The atelier creates written prompts; it does not render character images.
Locks & preservation
Tap the lock beside a trait to keep it unchanged during rerolls. Identity Lock keeps defining anatomy, face, hair, eyes, physique and identity accessory. Full Randomize preserves locks by default; Clear All Locks is explicit.
