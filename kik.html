Realm Record 001

A continent held together by fragile vows.

The Gilded Realms were once governed by a single celestial court. After the Starfall War, the old empire divided into five kingdoms, each preserving a different fragment of the original covenant.

Trade routes remain open, but spies, pilgrims, noble envoys, and wandering adventurers now move through a world where every alliance carries a hidden cost.

Key Locations

Aurelia

The Golden Archive

The official repository of imperial law, dynastic records, sealed prophecies, and censored histories.

  • Primary source of historical evidence.
  • Restricted vaults require royal authorization.
  • Archivists remember every visitor.

Valedorn

Starwatch Citadel

A fortified observatory where astronomer-knights track celestial omens and maintain the northern beacon network.

  • Reveals prophecy-related route flags.
  • Home of the Order of the White Stag.

Thornmere

The Echoing Grove

A living sanctuary said to preserve memories spoken beneath its silver-leafed trees.

  • Restores lost memories at a personal cost.
  • Changes based on the player's past choices.
  • Fae witnesses may repeat earlier dialogue.

Solmara

The Sun Court

A merchant parliament disguised as a royal palace, governed by contracts, favors, and public spectacle.

  • Hosts trade negotiations and formal duels.
  • Gold can substitute for reputation.
  • Public failures carry permanent consequences.

The Sealed Prophecy

The Testament of the Divided Star

When five crowns gather beneath a sunless dawn, the heir without a kingdom shall choose whether the old world is restored, remade, or permitted to vanish.

The prophecy survives in five incomplete translations. Each kingdom interprets the “heir without a kingdom” differently, and every court has built policy around its preferred reading.

◆

Known fragments: the northern verse speaks of blood, the eastern verse of memory, the western verse of sacrifice, the southern verse of debt, and the central verse of choice.
