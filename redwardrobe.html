The Wardrobe Philosophy

Fashion before randomness. Each look starts with a single clear subculture-inspired silhouette, translated into a beautiful medieval-fantasy ensemble.

A midnight academy scholar has an entirely different wardrobe architecture from a funeral duchess, forest herbalist, vampire dandy, gothic maid, royal lancer, or witch of the old woods. Curated details stay with their own design family.

For particularly consistent characters, keep the same fashion identity and use Outfit Set rerolls rather than Full Fashion Shuffle.
