The Wardrobe Philosophy

01 · Role first. A prince dresses differently from a commoner. Each social role has its own tailored outfit sets.

02 · Aesthetic second. Royalcore, Ouji, Gothic Aristocrat, Mori, Romantic Goth and other fashion languages are adapted into appropriate medieval garments.

03 · Feminine & masculine silhouettes. Petite adult women, tall adult men, and beautiful JRPG character proportions.

04 · Fashion over clutter. Purposeful layering, elegant craftsmanship, flattering shapes, beautiful colors and signature accessories.

05 · Portrait priority. Face, collar, upper-body couture, gestures, hairstyle and headwear are composed for expressive close-ups.
