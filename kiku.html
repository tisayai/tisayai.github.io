The hidden island of Kikuya

Beyond the mainland lies a secluded realm where women govern, ancient houses prosper, and the island’s rarest sons are guarded like living treasures.

1

Island and Mainland

The Mainland

  • A vast continent of ordinary kingdoms, ports, and provincial cities.
  • Its customs follow familiar social traditions and hereditary rule.
  • Most trade goods and nearly all foreign-born men arrive from its shores.
  • Only licensed ships may approach Kikuya’s waters.

The Island

  • A mountainous sanctuary separated from the outer world.
  • Births of boys are exceptionally rare and closely recorded.
  • Local law, inheritance, and ceremony developed in isolation.
  • Visitors are watched from arrival until departure.
2

Society of the Island

  • Only a small number of boys are born in each generation.
  • Women inherit property, businesses, titles, and political authority.
  • Men are protected by strict law and considered vital to the island’s future.
  • Many eligible men are educated within the refined houses of Yoshiwara.
  • Private arrangements unite romance, prestige, duty, and political influence.
  • Family survival often outweighs personal desire, though the two frequently collide.
3

Key Locations

Yoshiwara

The lantern district at the island’s heart

  • A ceremonial quarter, pleasure district, and commercial center.
  • Visitors arrive seeking companionship, heirs, alliances, or social prestige.
  • Its gates, bridges, tea houses, and gardens are governed by old custom.
  • Many celebrated courtesans spend most of their adult lives within its walls.

The House of Kikuya

A prestigious residence of music and ceremony

  • The principal setting of the chronicle.
  • A high-ranking house known for refined male courtesans.
  • Managed through careful discipline, etiquette, and political discretion.
  • Its inner rooms conceal rivalries, loyalties, debts, and forbidden affection.

A Realm Behind the Mist

Kikuya is shaped by scarcity, ritual, desire, and inherited power. Its women command commerce and law, while its rare men stand at the center of family ambition and private longing. Beyond the guarded harbor, the wider world remains vast, unnamed, and full of possibility.
